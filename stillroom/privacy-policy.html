Stillroom Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026. This policy covers Stillroom for iPhone and iPad, provided by Guangshun Wang. Privacy contact: devxiu6@gmail.com.

In a few words

Stillroom is an offline mood room. Your mood check-ins, note selections, favorites and preferences are saved locally. We do not receive these memories. There is no account, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking or social sharing service in the app.

Data on your device

The app stores dates and mood labels you select, identifiers of daily notes and notes already seen, favorite note identifiers, and language, sound, touch feedback and reduced-motion preferences. This powers your daily room, notes and history. The app requests no camera, microphone, location, contacts or health permissions and does not use HealthKit. Notes and room sounds are bundled with the app; there is no server-based AI conversation.

Retention, backups and deletion

Records remain until you change them, clear your memories or remove the app. In Room preferences choose "Erase all memories" and confirm to remove this installation's moods, favorites and preferences. There is no server account to delete. Removing the app normally removes its active local data. The operating system may include app data in device backups depending on your settings. Stillroom does not provide cloud sync or manage those backups. Clearing app data does not remove backup copies; restoring a backup may restore earlier records. Manage backups in Apple's device or iCloud settings.

Optional website visits and support email

Core features work offline. Opening a privacy or support link uses your browser and network connection. These static pages use GitHub Pages hosting. The hosting provider may process technical request information, including IP addresses, for security and delivery. See GitHub's privacy statement. We add no website analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies or contact forms.

If you email support, we receive the email address and message you choose to send. Gmail processes this correspondence. We use it to respond to your request and retain it only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Avoid sending sensitive personal or medical information. Request access, correction or deletion of correspondence at the same address. We cannot access local mood memories unless you choose to include information in your message.

Sharing and your choices

We do not sell or rent personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or transmit local moods to advertising, analytics or AI providers. Support correspondence may be processed by the email provider and disclosed when legally required. Providers may process information outside your country under their policies. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of information we hold and complain to a relevant privacy authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights. We cannot retrieve records held only on your device.

Children, security and scope

This is a general-audience companion, not a service directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app. Parents may contact us about email sent to support. Local files benefit from iOS app sandboxing and device security; no system guarantees absolute security. Stillroom provides companionship and personal reflection, not medical diagnosis, treatment, therapy or emergency support.

Changes and contact

We update this page and its effective date when our practices change. Material changes involving new collection will be reflected in the app or store information before that practice begins. Contact devxiu6@gmail.com for privacy questions.
