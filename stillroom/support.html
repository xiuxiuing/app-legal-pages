Stillroom Help and Support

A quiet offline room for the day you are having. Stillroom is provided by Guangshun Wang.

Contact

Email devxiu6@gmail.com for help and privacy requests. Include your app version, iOS version and a short description of the issue. Avoid sending private mood histories or medical information.

How to use Stillroom

In Room, choose how you feel today. Your mood changes the atmosphere and selects an original short note. "Another note" shows a different note; "Keep this" saves it. Kept notes holds favorites, and My days holds dated check-ins. Tap your companion, choose Come closer or rest together. There are no streaks to maintain.

Sound, language and motion

Room sound is optional and off by default. Turn it on in Room preferences; sound respects device silent behavior and mixes with other audio. Choose English, Simplified Chinese or your system language. System Reduce Motion is respected and an app-level Reduce motion switch is also available.

Your data

Memories are local, without accounts or app-operated cloud sync. If a save or load fails, the app displays a retry message and preserves an unreadable file. Clear all memories only when you want to reset them; the app cannot undo this. Device backups are controlled by Apple and may restore older records. See the privacy policy.

Free and offline

This version is free, without ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Core room, notes, favorites and history work offline. Opening web links or sending email requires a connection. Stillroom is not a medical or emergency service.
