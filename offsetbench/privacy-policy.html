OffsetBench Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026.

Support

This policy describes OffsetBench, an offline two-point conduit-offset calculator, its support website and support correspondence. The App Store market is the United States. Developer: Guangshun Wang. Privacy and support contact: devxiu6@gmail.com.

Calculations and local storage

You enter dimensions, angles, tool parameters, source descriptions, project labels and saved calculations. These are stored in app-local storage, including drafts, history and favorites. The current app does not automatically upload these records to the developer. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Accounts, analytics and permissions

The current app has no developer account registration, advertising, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics SDK, location access or camera access. It does not read your existing clipboard. Copying a result replaces clipboard content only when you request it. Apple may provide platform-level services and app diagnostics according to your device choices and Apple's policies; the developer has not added an automatic diagnostics uploader to the app.

Local experiment counts

Version 1.0.1 can keep an assigned Pro introduction and counts of app and purchase interactions on your device. No experiment data is uploaded automatically and no analytics SDK is included. You can disable and clear these counts in Guide. Resetting calculations does not change your assigned introduction or purchase rights. An optional shared local summary contains event counts and the assigned introduction, not calculation dimensions, tool names or project content.

Apple purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases and restoration. The app reads verified StoreKit transactions to determine access to Pro. The developer does not receive payment-card information through the app. Version 1.0.0 offers a one-time Pro purchase. Version 1.0.1 introduces an optional weekly subscription and an introductory trial for eligible Apple Accounts, subject to App Store approval and availability. Apple manages trial eligibility, billing, cancellation and refunds. A lifetime purchase does not cancel an existing subscription automatically.

Sharing

When you choose to copy or export, the chosen clipboard or sharing destination may receive the text you select. Review job-site or customer information before sharing. Those destinations operate under their own policies.

Website and support email

The legal/support website is hosted through GitHub Pages and support email is handled through Gmail. Visiting the website or sending email involves those providers, which may process technical connection data or the message you send. This is separate from the app's local calculation storage. Support messages are used to respond to your request; do not include sensitive customer, job-site, payment or account information.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains until you delete individual records, reset local data, or remove the app subject to Apple's device/backup behavior. Apple manages purchase history separately. Contact the developer to request access to or deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable obligations and the email provider's controls.

US users and privacy requests

The storefront is the United States. Contact the developer at the email above with privacy questions. Applicable rights and requirements depend on the relevant law and the developer's circumstances.
