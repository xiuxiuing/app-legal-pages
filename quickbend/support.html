Common questions

Does QuickBend require an account?

No. QuickBend does not require an account, login, or server connection for calculator features.

Are Jobs and Custom Benders uploaded?

No. Jobs, notes, bender presets, inputs, and results stay on the device. QuickBend does not provide cloud restore for local records.

How do I restore QuickBend Pro?

Open Settings or the QuickBend Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. Apple verifies the StoreKit entitlement. If Pro remains unavailable, confirm the Apple ID that made the purchase and contact support without sending account credentials.

Does QuickBend include ads, analytics, or tracking?

No. The current version has no third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Why test on scrap?

Take-up, gain, radius, shoe markings, conduit material, wear, and jobsite setup can vary. Verify bender markings and make a scrap test before production bends. QuickBend is a calculation aid, not a replacement for a licensed electrician or local safety/code judgment.
