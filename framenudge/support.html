How do I start?

Choose People, Landscape, Food or Architecture, then choose a composition. Follow one suggestion at a time. The shutter does not require perfect alignment.

How do backlit scenes work?

Choose subject or background exposure priority and tap the area whose detail matters most. This is a trade-off, not a guarantee that bright and dark areas can both be preserved. Your manual brightness adjustment takes priority.

Are photos or faces uploaded?

No. Frames and temporary face rectangles are processed on-device, without identity recognition. The app has no account or cloud inference. Email attachments are sent only if you choose to send them.

A photo did not save. What should I do?

Open Photos & recovery. Keep the app installed. Retry a failed save after checking add-only Photos permission, or export the local file. If the state is uncertain, check Photos first; a retry can create a duplicate. The app stops new capture after three unresolved photos.

Can I shoot stars or light trails?

Version 1.0 does not include star-trail or light-trail stacking, RAW, video or Apple's native Night mode. Low-light hints help you notice difficult conditions; they cannot replace a tripod or advanced image processing.

Camera or motion guide unavailable?

Check permissions, close other camera sessions, then retry. Available functions depend on the device. Motion data may be unavailable; composition references remain useful. Simulators are not real cameras.

What should I send to support?

Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, steps and visible error. Do not send private photos, passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information. Never delete unresolved photos merely to reproduce a bug.
