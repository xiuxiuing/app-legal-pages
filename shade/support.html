Shade Support

Set up the extension

  1. In iPhone Settings, find Safari → Extensions and enable Shade. On newer versions, Safari is under Apps.
  2. In Safari, open an ordinary HTTP or HTTPS webpage and select Shade in the page/extension menu.
  3. Allow access to the website when Safari asks. Choose the current domain as one of your three free selections, or use verified Pro access.
  4. Choose Automatic or Force dark. Automatic may keep the original when the system is light or the site is already dark.

A page looks wrong

Choose Restore this page now for a temporary restore, or Keep original to save an exception. A temporary restore does not save a rule and may end on reload. If saving fails or the panel is unavailable, disable Shade in Safari and reload. Do not buy Pro just to fix a broken page.

Settings do not update

Return to Safari, open the extension panel or reload the page. The app cannot push changes directly to every background Safari page. A recent response in Shade is not proof that all websites have permission.

Free websites and purchases

The three free selections count registered domains and their subdomains together. You can remove a selection to choose another. Pro still requires Safari permission. Use Restore purchases in Shade with the correct Apple account; purchase/restore requires a connection. Apple manages refunds. Do not send payment card details to support.

Delete local choices

In Shade Settings, delete saved websites or reset appearance. This does not cancel your Apple purchase, revoke Safari permission or erase copies in system backups. Manage those separately with Apple and your device controls.

Clear the extension observation

In Settings, choose Clear extension observation and confirm. Only the last response time is removed; the overview returns to Not yet verified. This does not disable Shade, revoke Safari permissions, delete sites or remove purchases. A later permitted non-private panel response can record a new observation.

在“设置”选择“清除扩展观察记录”并确认，仅清除最近一次响应时间，使总览恢复“尚未验证”；不停用扩展、不撤销 Safari 权限、不删除站点或购买权益。之后获允许的非私密面板响应可能生成新记录。

Preview minimal diagnostics

In Shade, choose Settings → Support. Review the version and fixed local error-code details before tapping Copy diagnostic details. The report does not include websites, page contents or purchase identifiers. Copying replaces the current clipboard; Shade requests local-only storage with a ten-minute expiration and never reads the previous clipboard. Nothing is uploaded or emailed automatically. You decide whether and where to paste the report.

可在“设置 → 支持”预览并主动复制最小诊断，不含网站、网页内容或购买标识。复制会替换当前剪贴板，并请求仅本机保存、十分钟后过期；不会读取原有剪贴板内容，不自动上传或发送邮件。粘贴位置由你决定。
