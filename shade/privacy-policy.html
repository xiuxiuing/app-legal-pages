Shade Privacy Policy

Proposed publisher: Guangshun Wang. Privacy contact: devxiu6@gmail.com. This draft describes the current iPhone app and its Safari extension. It is not a statement that the app has been released.

1. The short version

Shade changes webpage colors on your iPhone, on websites that you allow in Safari. There is no Shade account, developer-operated processing server, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK. Shade does not upload webpage content, your saved website rules or browsing history to the developer.

2. Temporary webpage processing

To decide whether and how to change colors, the extension processes the current website domain, webpage structure and computed styles on your device. Safari may provide the current page address as extension context; Shade uses its domain and does not store its path, search terms or query string. Styles and limited element markers are temporary. Shade does not save webpage text, passwords, form values, screenshots or a list of the pages you visit, and does not read your existing clipboard contents.

Website permission permits the extension to read and modify an allowed webpage. It is important to understand that local processing is still access to that page; we do not claim that the extension is technically unable to access webpages. Shade does not bypass Safari's permission controls. Private browsing behavior is controlled by Safari; private or unconfirmed-private contexts must not update the app's observation timestamp.

3. Information saved on your iPhone

  • Website domains and exceptions that you explicitly choose, including up to three selected free domains. Pro processing of other allowed sites does not automatically create a browsing log.
  • Your appearance mode, theme choices and configuration version.
  • Verified purchase status, the relevant Apple transaction identifier and verification time, to deliver or restore the non-consumable Pro purchase.
  • The most recent permitted response time from the extension panel, used to explain whether the app has ever observed an extension response. It is not a live permission check or a website activity log.

The containing app and its native extension share these settings in an iOS App Group database. They are protected by iOS sandbox and file-protection mechanisms. Settings may be included in device backups under your Apple and device settings. Shade does not provide its own cloud synchronization.

Optional local support diagnostics

Settings → Support lets you preview a minimal report containing only the app version, build number, iOS version, message protocol version and a fixed code for the most recent local settings error in the current app session. That error code is held in memory, not accumulated into a persistent log. The report excludes website domains, page contents, purchase identifiers and free-form error descriptions. Only when you tap Copy diagnostic details does Shade replace the clipboard with this report. It requests local-only clipboard storage with a ten-minute expiration and does not read what was on the clipboard. No report is sent automatically. If you paste it into another app or send it separately, the recipient and that app's practices apply.

你可在“设置 → 支持”预览最小诊断：应用版本、构建号、iOS 版本、消息协议版本，以及本次应用会话中最近的本地设置错误代码。错误码只在内存保留，不形成持续日志。报告不含网站域名、网页内容、购买标识或任意错误描述。只有主动点击“复制诊断信息”才会替换剪贴板，并请求仅本机保存、十分钟后过期；不会读取原有剪贴板内容或自动发送。你自行粘贴或发送到其他应用后，接收方及该应用的隐私规则适用。

4. Why information is used

Local information is used only to apply your selected appearance rules, enforce the free website allowance, deliver verified purchases, and explain setup or failures. It is not used to build advertising profiles, sell data or analyze which sites you visit. The current build has no optional usage-summary export; any future data-practice change will require updated disclosures before release.

Applicable bases for local processing and any consent requirements must be reviewed for the actual distribution regions. Granting a device permission is not a waiver of privacy rights.

5. Apple and other providers

Apple distributes the app and handles payments, purchase verification, restoration and refunds. Shade does not receive payment card details. Apple may provide aggregate store reports or diagnostics under its own policies and your settings.

Public legal and support pages are static, without advertising or analytics scripts, remote fonts or contact forms. Their hosting provider may receive standard request details such as IP address, browser information and access time to deliver the page. Websites you browse independently process their own requests; Shade does not control their privacy practices.

If you choose to email support, the developer and the email service provider receive your address, message and any attachments you send. Do not send passwords, full browsing histories, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information. Shade does not send support reports automatically.

6. Sharing and international processing

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Developer-held support information may be processed by the email provider or disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect legal rights.

[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Email and hosting services may process information outside your country. The actual providers, locations and any required transfer safeguards must be confirmed before this draft becomes effective; this draft does not claim that a particular data-processing agreement or transfer clause has already been signed.

7. Retention

Chosen website rules remain locally until you delete them, remove the app or restore other settings that replace them. Offloading an app may preserve its data. Appearance preferences can be reset. Purchase records are retained locally to preserve access and restoration; deleting website rules does not erase or refund your Apple purchase. The last extension-response timestamp is replaced by a newer permitted observation rather than accumulated into an activity history.

System backup copies are managed separately by you and Apple. We cannot remotely erase data held only on your device or copies in your backups. Apple independently retains purchase records according to its policies.

[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Proposed support retention is deletion or de-identification within 12 months after resolving the request, unless further retention is required by law or a documented dispute. The publisher must approve and implement this process before making this promise effective.

8. Your controls and rights

You can remove saved domains and exceptions in Shade, reset appearance settings, stop conversion for a website, or revoke website/extension access in Safari. The temporary “Restore this page now” action does not itself delete a saved rule. Safari permissions, app data, backups and Apple purchases are separate controls.

For information the developer actually holds, contact devxiu6@gmail.com. Depending on applicable law, your rights may include access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, non-discrimination and a complaint to the relevant regulator. We may ask for proportionate verification. We will respond within applicable legal time limits. You do not need to send us your website list to make a request.

[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Region-specific rights, exceptions, contact address, representatives and any mandatory request channels must be confirmed by qualified counsel. This draft does not invent a company registration address, DPO or regional representative.

9. Cookies and tracking

The app and extension do not set advertising cookies or add tracking technologies. A website may use its own cookies, and Apple or hosting services may process information under their own policies. Shade does not add an analytics or advertising SDK.

10. Security

Local data relies on iOS protections and your device security. No system is perfectly secure. Keep your iPhone updated and protect access to it. We do not promise end-to-end encryption, independent penetration testing or other controls that have not been implemented and verified.

11. Children

[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED] Shade is not intended as a child-directed service or a Kids Category app. The publisher must confirm the appropriate age disclosures and regional requirements. We do not intentionally solicit children's information through support; a parent or guardian may contact us about a message a child sent. Purchases and parental controls are handled by Apple.

12. Changes

Material changes will be reflected in the policy and App Store disclosures before a release changes data practices. Where applicable law requires additional notice or consent, those requirements will apply. This draft has no effective date until it is approved for publication.

Separate observation deletion

In Settings → Clear extension observation, you can confirm deletion of the last locally stored extension-response time. This returns the overview to Not yet verified without deleting websites, changing appearance, removing verified purchases or revoking Safari permissions. A later permitted non-private panel response can record a new observation. Existing system backups are managed separately by Apple and your device settings.
